Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2, 2026

Search is a web browser developed by DevBangs. Our approach is simple: your browsing stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. Search does not collect, store, or transmit your personal information to us or any third party. There is no account and no sign-up.

Stored on your device only

To work as a browser, Search keeps the following locally on your device. This never leaves your phone:

  • Browsing history (not recorded in Night Owl private mode)
  • Bookmarks you save
  • Your settings and preferences
  • Cookies and site data, managed by the system WebView
  • Files you choose to download

You can delete all of this any time via Settings → Clear browsing data.

Permissions

Search uses only what it needs: internet access to load pages; location, camera, and microphone only when a website requests them and you allow it; and storage for downloads you choose.

Third-party websites

When you visit a website, that site's own privacy practices apply. Search offers ad blocking and a private mode to reduce tracking, but no browser can fully prevent third-party tracking.
