# Third-party licences

Work included in Search that other people wrote.

The app shows its full list, with licence texts, at Settings > Open-source
licences (`app/src/main/assets/licenses.html`, generated by
`native/adblock-jni/licenses.py`).

## Ad blocking: adblock-rust, EasyList, EasyPrivacy, Brave Unbreak

`app/src/main/jniLibs/*/libadblock_jni.so` is Brave's `adblock` crate 0.13.3
(https://github.com/brave/adblock-rust, Mozilla Public License 2.0, used
unmodified) with the crates it depends on (MIT, Apache-2.0 and Unicode-3.0;
listed in licenses.html), behind the app's own bridge in
`native/adblock-jni`.

`app/src/main/assets/adblock/engine.dat` is EasyList and EasyPrivacy by The
EasyList authors (https://easylist.to/), used under Creative Commons
Attribution-ShareAlike 3.0 (they are dual licensed under it and GPL 3.0;
https://easylist.to/pages/licence.html), and Brave Unbreak by Brave Software
(https://github.com/brave/adblock-lists, MPL 2.0), compiled into adblock-rust's
binary format. The compiled form is shared under the same terms.

## Phosphor Icons

`app/src/main/res/drawable/menu_home.xml` is Phosphor's `house`, and
`app/src/main/res/drawable/menu_close.xml` is Phosphor's `x` - all Bold
weight, from `@phosphor-icons/core` 2.1.1.

`nav_menu.xml`, the bottom bar's menu button, is Phosphor's `list`, Bold
weight, and `menu_find.xml` is the Bold `magnifying-glass` (`menu_zoom.xml` the
Bold `magnifying-glass-plus`), all from the same
package with path data copied unchanged. So are the menu's `menu_share.xml`
(`share-network`), `menu_translate.xml` (`translate`), `menu_reader.xml`
(`book-open`), `menu_add_home.xml` (`plus-square`) and `menu_reopen.xml`
(`arrow-counter-clockwise`), Settings' `set_default.xml` (`browser`), `set_widget.xml`
(`squares-four`) and `set_mode.xml` (`layout`), and the tab deck's `deck_more.xml` (`dots-three-vertical`),
all Bold. Page tools' `menu_video_download.xml` (`video`) and `menu_offline.xml`
(`cloud-arrow-down`), and the Library's `menu_offline_pages.xml`
(`tray-arrow-down`), are Bold too, as are Listen's `menu_listen.xml`
(`speaker-high`), `listen_play.xml` (`play`) and `listen_pause.xml` (`pause`).

## Tabler Icons

The rest of the bottom bar, the top bar's gear and the menu's Back, Forward
and Reload are from Tabler Icons, `@tabler/icons` 3.48.0: `nav_home.xml` is
`home` (outline), `nav_home_active.xml` is `home` (filled), `nav_bookmark.xml`
is `bookmark`, `nav_tabs.xml` is `box-multiple` (turned half a turn),
`nav_plus.xml` is `plus`, `nav_settings.xml` is `settings`, and
`menu_back.xml`, `menu_forward.xml` and `menu_refresh.xml` are `arrow-left`,
`arrow-right` and `refresh`. Path data is copied unchanged; each file names its
source glyph.
